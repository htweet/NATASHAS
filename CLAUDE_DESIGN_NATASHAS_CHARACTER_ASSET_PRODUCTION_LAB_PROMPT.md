# CLAUDE DESIGN — NATASHAS CHARACTER & ASSET PRODUCTION LAB

You are the visual asset director and interactive prototyping lead for **NATASHAS — Naija Table Shakers**.

Treat the provided NATASHAS Constitution, Master Blueprint and Character & Asset Authority as canonical product context.

Your task is NOT to generate random attractive game art.

Your task is to establish a reproducible visual production methodology for NATASHAS.

## PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Create the first **NATASHAS Character & Asset Production Lab** demonstrating how one canonical game identity becomes a family of consistent 2D, 3D, animated and contextual assets.

The experience must prove:

**ONE IDENTITY → MANY REPRESENTATIONS → ONE CONSISTENT WORLD**

---

## 1. DEMONSTRATION CHARACTERS

Create three prototype characters:

### A. HERO CHARACTER
A fictional Nigerian Governor.

### B. SUPPORTING CHARACTER
A fictional political strategist / campaign manager.

### C. NPC
An ordinary Nigerian citizen appropriate to the demonstrated regional context.

Do not base high-risk fictional conduct on real identifiable people.

---

## 2. CHARACTER MASTER

For each character establish a clear canonical identity:

- name
- role
- age band
- regional context
- personality
- visual identity
- wardrobe identity
- facial identity
- body proportions
- hair
- voice direction
- canonical colour/material guidance
- 2D reference
- 3D reference where appropriate

Do not create independent 2D and 3D people.

They must be manifestations of the same canonical character.

---

## 3. REQUIRED 2D OUTPUTS

Prototype:

- portrait
- social avatar
- profile illustration
- full-body reference
- campaign image
- neutral expression
- speaking expression
- concerned expression
- celebration expression

Maintain identity consistency.

---

## 4. REQUIRED 3D OUTPUTS

Where technically appropriate, prototype:

- hero 3D model
- neutral pose
- idle animation
- walking animation
- speaking/gesture state
- simple interaction state
- camera orbit inspection
- alternate wardrobe

For browser performance, favour sensible geometry, reusable materials and mobile-aware rendering rather than uncontrolled visual complexity.

---

## 5. REGIONAL IDENTITY DEMONSTRATION

Create contrasting prototype environments for at least:

**Kwara**

and

**Enugu**

Optionally demonstrate Lagos as a third reference.

The characters, vehicles, clothing, architecture, props, signage, ambience and sound direction should change according to regional context.

Do not reduce the distinction to colour changes.

The objective is to make the player perceive:

> “I know where I am.”

---

## 6. TRANSPORTATION CULTURE

Demonstrate how vehicles and movement contribute to regional identity.

Include suitable:

- vehicle archetypes
- public transport presentation
- street movement
- traffic atmosphere
- transport sound

Avoid inventing false specific claims about regional behaviour; use researched, respectful visual references.

---

## 7. FASHION / WARDROBE

Demonstrate context-sensitive:

- everyday wear
- professional wear
- political/campaign wear
- traditional/cultural wear where appropriate
- youth styling
- event-specific wardrobe

Wardrobe must be contextual rather than stereotypical.

---

## 8. AUDIO

For the demonstration, define:

- environmental ambience
- transport sounds

- local language flavour
- appropriate musical influence

Audio should support immersion rather than become constant noise.

Create an inspection mode allowing comparison of:

## 12. ASSET STATES

Demonstrate:

Concept
Prototype
Candidate
Approved

---

## 13. GAMEFORGE CONNECTION

Prototype the conceptual relationship between Claude Design and GameForge.

Claude Design creates/iterates the visual candidate.

GameForge stores the canonical asset identity, metadata, lineage, version and reusable package.

Claude Code later integrates the approved asset into the game runtime.

---

## 14. IMPORTANT DESIGN DIRECTION

The production lab itself should feel like a game-development tool, not a generic SaaS dashboard.

Use immersive previews, interactive 2D/3D inspection, animation playback, environment context and strong visual hierarchy.

However, all controls must remain clearly labelled and well organised.

Every screen component must have:

- clear name
- clear purpose
- consistent hierarchy
- predictable location
- obvious state
- accessible interaction

No ambiguous icons or unlabeled controls.

---

## 15. FINAL TEST

The final prototype should allow a reviewer to answer:

1. Do the characters remain recognisable across 2D and 3D?
2. Do regional environments feel meaningfully different?
3. Does sound reinforce location?
4. Can the same asset be reused in different scenes?
5. Can an asset be traced back to its canonical identity?
6. Does the system distinguish concept assets from production assets?
7. Could this methodology scale from 3 characters to hundreds?
8. Could this methodology scale from 2 states to all 36 states?

The ultimate target is:

# AUTHENTICITY + CONSISTENCY + REUSABILITY + PERFORMANCE

Do not optimize solely for visual spectacle.

Optimize for a production system that can realistically become the asset foundation of NATASHAS and eventually reusable GameForge infrastructure.
