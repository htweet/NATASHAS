Regional Identity Rule
"This feels like where I am." — not merely "This says where I am." A player should be able to feel the regional shift without reading the location chip.
Transportation Culture
● Kwara Transport
Keke napep — primary short-distance
Danfo minibus — inter-city routes
Okada — motorcycle taxi, informal
Bolekaja — long-bench commercial bus
Donkey cart — rural market routes
Colour: Yellow + black stripe (Keke)
Sound: 2-stroke engine · road dust · horn
● Enugu Transport
Yellow Peugeot 504 — iconic city taxi
Buses — fixed route, numbered
Okada — motorcycle taxi
Keke napep — newer addition
Hiace minivan — interstate travel
Colour: Yellow body (iconic taxi)
Sound: Diesel engine · highland road
"Vehicles and movement contribute to regional identity." — Transport assets, street atmosphere, and movement culture must be distinct per region. A Kwara keke napep and an Enugu yellow Peugeot 504 are not interchangeable.
