# ARTICLE 1 — IDENTITY AND VISION

NATASHAS is a living, persistent Nigerian political ecosystem in which players participate in an evolving simulated republic.

It is not merely an election game. It combines:

- politics
- governance
- elections
- economics
- business
- social relationships
- media
- communication
- public sentiment
- institutions
- crises
- AI-assisted strategy
- emergent narratives
- tournaments
- leaderboards
- seasons
- player history
- cultural immersion

The world should continue to evolve when individual players are offline.

### North-star experience

> **Make a deeply complex simulation feel immediately understandable and continuously actionable.**

---

# ARTICLE 2 — CORE DESIGN PRINCIPLE

## COMPLEXITY WITHOUT CONFUSION

The simulation may contain highly sophisticated algorithms and interconnected systems.

Players should experience complexity through meaningful decisions and consequences, not through confusing navigation or excessive manual reading.

The interface should continuously answer:

- What is happening?
- What matters now?
- What changed?
- What can I do?
- What might happen if I do it?
- What happened because I acted?

---

# ARTICLE 3 — CORE GAME LOOP

The fundamental NATASHAS loop is:

**POWER → CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE → REACTION → OPPORTUNITY → NEW CHOICE**

The world should continuously produce meaningful choices rather than requiring players to follow a rigid quest sequence.

---

# ARTICLE 4 — REAL NIGERIA, FICTIONAL POLITICAL UNIVERSE

NATASHAS is deeply inspired by Nigeria and may use:

- the 36-state structure
- Nigerian geography and regional identities
- Nigerian cultural references
- languages and linguistic flavour
- music traditions
- social humour and memes
- local commerce
- transportation themes
- political structures as broad inspiration
- economic themes
- community dynamics

Potentially defamatory or high-risk allegations, criminal acts, corruption, violent events and conspiracies should primarily be attached to fictional characters, fictional parties, fictional institutions and fictional organisations.

NATASHAS is a fictional entertainment/simulation product and must not be presented as factual political reporting.

---

# ARTICLE 5 — FICTIONAL-SIMULATION NOTICE

Every first-time visit must present a prominent notice before the player enters the game.

## ⚠️ BEFORE YOU ENTER

This is a fictional political strategy and social simulation game inspired by Nigerian society, politics and culture.

All political characters, parties, governments, events, conspiracies and outcomes within the game are fictional or simulated for entertainment.

Gameplay should not be interpreted as factual claims about real persons, organizations or governments.

The game does not encourage or facilitate real-world violence, criminal activity, political disruption, harassment, intimidation or interference with democratic processes.

## WHAT HAPPENS IN THE GAME STAYS IN THE GAME.

**[ I UNDERSTAND & ENTER ]**

A persistent indicator should identify the experience as:

**FICTIONAL SIMULATION**
